# Industry & talent partnerships

The University of Portsmouth AI Society connects technology companies, founders and recruiters with students through practical workshops, build sprints and project showcases. We’re a free, student-led UPSU society open to every course and skill level. Partners can meet students interested in AI, software and creative technology, from first-time builders to experienced GitHub contributors.

[Open the interactive partnership prospectus](https://uopaisociety.github.io/society/brand/partnerships/) · [View the HTML source](brand/partnerships/index.html)

## Partnership tracks

| Track | Best for | What we can deliver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Single-Event Pilot — The Build Night Drop** | A first collaboration with a clear result to review | Cover catering, micro-prizes or API credits worth **£100–£250** for one session. Includes Luma and GitHub co-branding, one registration CTA, a live introduction and a LinkedIn recap. |
| **Talent & Recruitment Pipeline** | Graduate employers, placement platforms and hiring teams | A semester or annual programme with signup opportunities through WhatsApp, Luma and relevant Gradcracker activity, a co-hosted employability or portfolio workshop, and opt-in introductions to contributors and committee members. |
| **Technical Ecosystem & DevRel** | Founders, AI toolmakers and cloud or developer platforms | A semester or annual programme with a dedicated challenge or sprint, an open-source starter template, a speaker or judging seat, and opt-in beta testing or internship introductions. |

## How we work

We agree on one student action and one measurable outcome before promotion. Possible outcomes include verified signups, working demos or first successful API calls. We use Luma for registration and a partner-side report to verify any external conversions. A workshop repo, public showcase where projects are cleared for publication, and LinkedIn recap give the work a life beyond the session.

Once the date, CTA, artwork and any approvals are in place, we aim to go live on Luma, WhatsApp and GitHub within 48 hours.
