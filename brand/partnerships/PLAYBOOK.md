# Partnership meeting playbook

Committee working guide. This file lives in the public society repository. Keep contact details, commercial terms under discussion and private meeting notes in the committee’s private workspace.

## Run a 15-minute call

### 0–3 minutes · Discovery

Open with “Before I show you what we do, can I ask what would make this worth your time?” Ask one set of questions, then repeat back the single outcome you heard.

| Recruiter or careers platform | Founder or technical lead |
| --- | --- |
| “Which students are you most keen to reach this term?” | “What would you most like students to build or test?” |
| “What’s the one student action that would make a pilot worthwhile for you?” | “What would be most valuable after one session: working demos, product feedback or people you’d want to meet again?” |
| “How would you verify that action, and what result would make you run it again?” | “What would a good result look like, and who on your team could help us judge it?” |

### 3–8 minutes · Show the work

1. Open the [GitHub organisation](https://github.com/uopaisociety). Show one relevant starter repo or project and open its README. Explain what a student can run, change and share. Use work already in the repo and label any proposed build as an example.
2. Open the [prospectus](https://uopaisociety.github.io/society/brand/partnerships/#roi) at Slide 6. For offline use, download [index.html](index.html) and open the file in a browser. Type the company or contact name in **Custom meeting mode**. Click **Present** or press **P** outside the input. Use the arrow keys to move between slides.
3. Pick the persona and point to the metric they mentioned. Use **Talent & Recruitment Partner** for signups or profiles, **Founder / Technical Builder** for demos or introductions, and **AI Tooling / DevRel Sponsor** for onboarding or API activation.
4. Agree who measures the action and the reporting window. A required Luma question records a response. The partner confirms external actions in aggregate. Keep partner introductions opt-in.

### 8–12 minutes · Close on one pilot

If a semester commitment is too early, say “We can try one Thursday first. You cover something the students can use, like pizza, credits or a small prize. We’ll agree on one thing to measure and show you the result before we talk about anything longer.”

Use **£100–£250 in cash or equivalent value** as the starting range. Offer one registration CTA, co-branding on Luma and GitHub, a room introduction and a recap. If they prefer credits or prizes, confirm the usable value and any expiry before booking.

### 12–15 minutes · Agree the next action

Agree who owns the event date, CTA, logo, metric and results report. Ask “Shall I send a short outline you can react to?” After the call, write down the action, owner and due date. Aim to publish within 48 hours of having the date, materials and any approvals.

## Call plans

### Jobortunity · Talent & Recruitment

Lead with a practical application workshop. Show how a student could take one project and turn it into a better application for a specific UK placement or graduate role. Use the **Talent & Recruitment Partner** setting on Slide 6.

- “Which UK placements or graduate roles do you want Portsmouth students to find on Jobortunity?”
- “Would you get more value from a verified signup, a completed profile or a student tracking their first application?”
- “Could you provide an aggregate count of that action from a society link within seven days?”
- **Proposed pilot** — Trial the student access with two or three committee members, then run one co-hosted portfolio-to-application workshop if the UK role coverage and access terms fit. Agree access duration, eligibility and any renewal terms. Use one attributed opt-in link and pick one result, such as verified profile completions in seven days.

[Jobortunity product page](https://jobortunity.ai/en)

### Alex Zhi · Founder / Technical Builder

Lead with a student-led creative challenge for Computing, Film, Animation and Games Design students. Show a starter repo that demonstrates how the society structures a short build, then switch to **Founder / Technical Builder** on Slide 6.

- “What would you most like students to make with Dreem Studio?”
- “Which would be most valuable after the session: working interactive stories, feedback from new creators or introductions to teams you’d like to work with?”
- “Could Dreem provide enough creator credits for one session and someone to judge the demos?”
- **Proposed pilot** — Mixed creative and technical teams build a short branching story, demo it live and give feedback on the creation process. Count demos that meet the agreed brief. Confirm credit limits and publication permissions with the partner, then offer opt-in follow-ups with interested teams.

[Dreem](https://www.dreem-world.ai/) · [Dreem’s interactive content challenge](https://luma.com/supr0anz)

## Outreach templates

Use a real detail in the first paragraph and one specific idea. Keep the prospectus link on its own line.

### Cold outbound

Hi [Name], I’m Carlos from the AI Society at Portsmouth. I saw [specific thing they’re working on] and thought we could try a [short pilot idea] with our students.

https://uopaisociety.github.io/society/brand/partnerships/

If that’s something you’re looking at, happy to chat for 15 minutes. Carlos

### Post-meeting follow-up

Hi [Name], good to speak today. I liked your point about [specific priority]. I’d suggest we try [one pilot] and see how many students [agreed action].

https://uopaisociety.github.io/society/brand/partnerships/

If that sounds about right, I’m happy to send over a date and a short outline. Carlos
